I’m really sorry.

For bothering you at night sometimes, for asking again and again, and for the things I left unsaid or said the wrong way. I know that can make a good day feel heavier.

I should listen better, ask once, respect your space, and be more careful with the promises I make. I know saying sorry matters less than showing it, so I’ll let my actions speak.

I’m not writing this to ask for an answer. I just wanted to say it properly, without rushing you.

— from your Buddhu

No reply is expected. Take your time, and take care.
